F1 2026: United States GP

About United States Grand Prix

The United States GP represents a thrilling chapter in the 2026 Formula 1 season at Circuit of the Americas. This edition holds special significance as it is the 19th round of the 2026 Formula 1 World Championship. Located in Austin, Texas, this iconic venue hosts the world's finest motorsport drivers for high-speed racing action.

Race Weekend Format

The United States GP weekend features a standard format. Friday opens with Free Practice 1 and Free Practice 2. Saturday hosts Free Practice 3 followed by Main Qualifying. Sunday's main Grand Prix features 56 laps of intense racing.

Circuit History

Circuit of the Americas (COTA) opened in 2012 and has since become a staple of the Formula 1 calendar. Known for its iconic 25-meter elevation climb into Turn 1, fast esses, and passionate American fans, COTA remains a premier destination in modern motorsport history.

Expected Participants

The 2026 Formula 1 grid features leading teams and drivers including McLaren's Lando Norris, Mercedes drivers Kimi Antonelli and George Russell, Ferrari's Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton, alongside Red Bull Racing's Max Verstappen.
